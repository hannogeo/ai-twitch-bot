A bot your chat will actually talk to.

Responds like a regular, not a script.

Set a personality in a sentence, add a short note about your regulars, and it handles the rest: greeting people, answering questions, keeping up with the conversation.

Signs in with your Twitch account.

One OAuth sign-in and it knows your channel. No token generator, no copying long strings. Want it to post as a bot instead? Sign in a second account.

Knows when to look things up.

For time-sensitive questions it searches the web and answers with current info rather than guessing.

Triggers by @mention, by command (!ai), or by reply, whichever your chat already does.

Up and running in a couple of minutes.

  1. 01

    Download the app

    Download the Windows installer and open it.

  2. 02

    Sign in with Twitch

    Authorize your streamer account and the bot finds your channel on its own.

  3. 03

    Add a free Groq key

    Get one at console.groq.com; it powers the replies.

  4. 04

    Press start

    The bot joins your chat and starts talking.
